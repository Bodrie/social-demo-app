# Review pass, 2026-10-19 (plan A4)

Completes the review of 2026-10-16 (`PRE_RELEASE_REVIEW_2026-10-16.md`): its four tracks that never
produced a report (accessibility beyond axe, shared-contract drift, ops and docs drift, test
coverage), each run as a read-only sweep and then fixed where the finding held, plus the Low items
of that review that were a few lines each. Findings are numbered per track; "fixed" means in this
pass, "open" means parked with a reason, "not a bug" means the sweep's suspicion did not survive a
read of the code.

## 5. Test coverage

The five tests the 2026-10-16 review asked for exist (H1 ×3, M3, H2). Every HTTP route and
every socket handler is exercised by an integration test. Closed in this pass:

- Gateway pushes for the group lifecycle (`conversation:update` to members on creation, on a
  newcomer and on a leaver; `conversation:removed` to the leaver).
- Error paths with no 4xx assertion: unknown username, revisions of an unknown post, clearing a
  reaction on an unknown comment, malformed `limit`/`cursor` query parameters, sign-out without a
  cookie.
- Web: the Turnstile widget failing to load, the throttled export toast, arrow keys in the
  mention list, the session error state with retry, a failed "sign out everywhere", a deliberate
  sign-out not remembering the page, older revisions on demand, the account export and deletion.
- Axe: `/u/:username/followers` and the new-message dialog.

Open, with reasons: `onProfileUpdated` presence rebroadcast and the ws branch of the exception
filter (needs a gateway harness for pushes that are not conversation events); e2e journeys for
"sign out everywhere", resend verification, group add/leave, emoji picker, cross-tab sync, the
`?comment=` deep link and the cover upload (each a few minutes of a future chat/profile slice);
flakiness risks noted by the sweep: the `POST /auth/password` describe sits exactly at the
route's 5/min budget (one more test there needs its own app), two fixed 200 ms sleeps used as
negative assertions, the 1 s token expiry in the chat spec, and the global 100/min throttler that
no test overrides. Seen in this pass: `route-error.test.tsx` failed once under full-suite load
(the reload spy counted zero calls) and passed on every rerun, alone and in pairs; if it recurs,
await the spy with `waitFor` instead of asserting right after the heading appears.

Found and fixed during B3a: `chat.spec.ts` "a group reaches every member live" missed "Seen by
everyone" in every full run once two more alice journeys existed, while it passed alone. The
trace showed all of alice's socket handshakes answered `Too many connections`: every worker is
signed in as alice, so the per-user limits meant for one person's tabs (30 handshakes a minute,
5 open sockets) were exhausted by the parallel run; her message still went out over HTTP, but
bob's read receipt is a push and never arrived. The limits are now environment variables
(`SOCKET_CONNECTS_PER_MINUTE`, `SOCKET_MAX_PER_USER`) that the Playwright API raises. The same
run also showed `auth.spec.ts` filling the login page's Email field before the lazy
forgot-password route had replaced it (fixed by waiting for the page heading first).

Closed on 2026-10-20 as not reproduced (seen once on 2026-10-19, never in the eight parallel
repeats of the file nor in any full run since): `comments.spec.ts` opened alice's comments section after bob's comment had been
confirmed on his page, yet the section rendered "No comments yet" (an empty 200, not an
error). Nothing in the client caches that list before the section opens, so the cause is
unknown; if it recurs, keep the trace (`--trace retain-on-failure`) and compare the timing of
bob's POST and alice's GET in the `*.network` log.

The first-paint bundle stands at 244.2 kB gz against the 245 kB budget after B3a; the next
feature that touches the entry chunk has to raise it with a reason or move something out.
